# PdfCraft TH

An unofficial Thai edition of PdfCraft, based on PdfCraft by the ArtCraft team. It is not made or
endorsed by them.

## What is different

- Thai text shows in the interface, in file names and in search boxes, using Thai fonts already
  installed on your computer.
- Add Text and Edit Text write Thai into a PDF with Sarabun or Noto Sans Thai when one of them is
  installed (both are open fonts). PDFs that do not embed a Thai font are shown with an installed
  Thai font.
- Scan & OCR reads Thai as well as English, on your own computer. A scanned page becomes searchable
  and its text can be copied; the picture of the page stays as it was.
- No community card, no Discord button and no ArtCraft logos. The app icon is our own: the word pdfคับ on a red page.

## Downloads

- Mac (Apple silicon): the `.dmg`.
- Windows (x64): the `.exe`, with a text file of the licences that go with it.

These files are not signed. The first time you open them, macOS and Windows will warn you. On a
Mac, right-click the app and choose Open. On Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway.

## Known limits

- OCR makes text searchable, not editable. It can misread letters, especially ข as บ on small print,
  so check names before you rely on them.
- The Windows build is made by GitHub's servers and has not been tried by hand on a Windows PC yet.
- Writing Thai into a PDF needs Sarabun or Noto Sans Thai to be installed.
