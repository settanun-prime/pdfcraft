PdfCraft TH app icon (pdfcraft.svg, pdfcraft-small.svg and every file derived from them in this
directory: pdfcraft-1024.png, pdfcraft.icns, pdfcraft.ico, hicolor/).

Original artwork made for PdfCraft TH, an unofficial Thai edition of PdfCraft, by the owner of
this fork with the help of Claude (Anthropic). It replaces the upstream app icon in this edition.
The outlines of the letters ก, P, D and F are traced from Sarabun Bold (SIL Open Font License 1.1,
Copyright The Sarabun Project Authors); that licence does not restrict artwork made with a font.
No ArtCraft mark is used.
